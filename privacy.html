What our internal tools access

Our internal dashboard is used only by LunX Inc staff to manage our own seller and advertising accounts. It accesses our own business data, such as sales totals, order status, inventory levels, fees, product listings, and advertising campaign performance.

The dashboard is not offered to other sellers or the public, and it does not request access to buyer personal information such as names, addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses.

How we use this information

  • To track sales, inventory, and profitability of our own products
  • To review and adjust our own advertising campaigns
  • To plan purchasing and restocking

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share data obtained from Amazon with any third party. Data is used only for the internal purposes listed above.

Storage and security

Data is stored on systems controlled by LunX Inc. Access is limited to authorized staff. API credentials are kept in a secure password manager, are never shared, and are rotated when required. We keep data only as long as needed for our business and accounting purposes, then delete it.

Customer information from orders

When you buy our products through a marketplace such as Amazon or eBay, the marketplace processes your order and payment under its own privacy policy. We only receive what the marketplace provides to fulfil and support your order, and we use it only for that purpose.

Your rights

You may ask to access or correct personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting the person in charge of the protection of personal information at lunaxcan@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
